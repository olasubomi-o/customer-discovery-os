# The Evidence Ladder

Rank everything a customer says or does. The higher the rung, the more it is grounded in behavior instead of stated opinion.

| Rung | Signal | Example | Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Compliment | "That sounds cool." | opinion |
| 2 | Complaint | "That's frustrating." | opinion |
| 3 | Repeated behavior | "This happens every Friday." | behavioral |
| 4 | Workaround | "I built a spreadsheet for this." | behavioral |
| 5 | Cost | "We spend five hours a week on this." | behavioral |
| 6 | Existing spend | "We already pay $500/month for something similar." | transactional |
| 7 | Commitment | "Can I try it?" (with a date, a person, a deposit of time or access) | commitment |
| 8 | Payment | "Where do I pay?" (money actually moves) | transactional |

Compliments are not evidence. Interest is not commitment.

Strong pain combines frequency x severity x urgency. A problem that happens constantly but barely matters is not worth building for. A problem that is severe but rare usually is not either.

## Classifying a statement
- Said it, did not do it: rung 1 or 2.
- Describes something that happened more than once: rung 3.
- Built or bought a patch themselves: rung 4.
- Gives a number for time, money or risk: rung 5. Check it is a real number from their experience, not a guess about the future.
- Pays for an alternative today: rung 6.
- Offers a concrete next step that costs them something (intro to the buyer, a pilot slot, data access, a deposit): rung 7. "Send me more info" is rung 1.
- Pays: rung 8.

## What the ledger enforces
- `Strong` needs: 5+ distinct interviews, a rung of 5 or higher, and evidence type behavioral, transactional or commitment.
- `Strong` willingness to pay needs rung 6 or higher.
- `Emerging` needs 2+ sources and rung 3 or higher.
- Online-only (market scan) evidence is secondary: rung 4 at most, never Strong.
