# Participant Outreach

Read `references/guardrails.md` first. Read `discovery/context.md`; ask for the target participant type and the problem area if missing.

## Write the invitation
Write a short outreach message inviting someone to a customer research interview.

Important: do not pitch the startup; do not mention features; do not make it sound like a sales call; make clear the founder is trying to learn from their experience; keep it under 120 words; ask for 20 to 30 minutes; make it personalized and respectful; do not overstate the importance of the research.

Give three versions: 1 LinkedIn DM, 2 email, 3 text or WhatsApp.

Strong positioning: instead of "I'd love to tell you about our revolutionary platform", use "I'm researching how operators currently handle X and would love to understand how your team approaches it."

## Also provide
- Where to find the right people beyond friends and family (communities, forums, alumni networks, existing customers of adjacent products, recruiting panels). Prefer people who recently had the problem over people who match a demographic.
- A screener: 3 behavioral questions that confirm the person had the problem recently (for example "When did you last do X?"), no pitch in the screener.
- Who NOT to recruit: friends who want to be nice, people who never had the problem, people who cannot buy or influence the decision.

## Rules
- Personalize each message with one true, specific reason you are writing to this person. Never invent a connection or claim a relationship that does not exist.
- Do not scrape contact lists or send bulk unsolicited messages. A founder messaging individuals they chose is fine; do not build mass-mailing tooling.
- Include the consent reminder: say how the conversation will be recorded or noted and ask permission when you start.
- This skill drafts. The founder sends. Do not send anything.

## Finish
Save drafts to `discovery/synthesis/outreach-drafts.md`. Run `python3 scripts/ddos.py check`. Hand off: interview-prep if there is no guide, interview-analysis after the first interview.
